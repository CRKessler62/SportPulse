# Sports 20/20

## See What Matters Next.

Sports 20/20 is a multi-sport platform designed to help fans understand more than just the score.

We bring together **scores, standings, statistics, playoff information and context** so fans can quickly understand where their team stands and what matters next.

---

## ⚾ MLB

Sports 20/20 MLB includes:

- Live and final game scores
- Game information
- Winning pitcher
- Losing pitcher
- Saves
- League standings
- American League and National League views
- League leaders
- Batting statistics
- Pitching statistics
- Defensive statistics
- Stolen bases
- Playoff picture
- Division races
- Wild Card races

**Note:** Pitchers are excluded from defensive statistics.
